Why OpenCode?

  • Open Source Foundation
    Audit the code, run it locally, and deploy it on your own infrastructure. You control your data and your development environment.
  • Terminal Native
    Works where you work. No context switching to web browsers or specialized IDE plugins. It's just another command in your workflow.
  • Bring Your Own Model
    Plug in OpenAI, Anthropic, or run local models via Ollama. Swap intelligence providers without changing your workflow.
